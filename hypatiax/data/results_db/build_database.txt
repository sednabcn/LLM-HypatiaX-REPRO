The database is built and loaded with the real JSON contents; it's the .db file in the cards, with the scripts that rebuild it.
Your zip matched checksums.txt byte for byte (0 mismatches). It holds 385 of the 410 JSONs, and I parsed 258 unique files. Spot checks of row counts against the raw JSON passed in all 18 cases I tried, and one DeFi test R² matched exactly.
What's in it
    • Pipeline and inventory: 40 experiments from run_all.sh, plus all 432 files with their checksums, duplicate links and parsed seed, shard, temperature and timestamp.
    • DeFi benchmark: 5,829 rows of case, system (pure LLM, neural net, hybrid) and R², success and time.
    • Feynman runs: 3,810 rows of test, method and R², RMSE, time and formula.
    • Nguyen-12: 960 per-equation results, run configs and summaries, and 64,616 PySR search-trajectory steps.
    • Other result tables:
        ◦ Ablation: 105 rows, plus wall-clock flags and instability indices.
        ◦ Sweeps: noise sweep and sample complexity.
        ◦ Portfolio variance: seed results.
        ◦ Audit: the contradiction-audit findings.
    • Everything else: every raw value of every parsed JSON (about 204,000), for formats without a typed table.
    • Views and examples: summary views, and queries.sql with example queries (all of them run).
What I found while loading
    • Duplicates: 152 of the 410 JSONs are byte-identical copies. All 25 files in extrapolation_sparse_seed_off are copies of extrapolation/. 41 of the fixc3_baseline* files and 33 of the checkpoint stubs are one identical file each.
    • The fallback folder differs: extrapolation-sparse_seeds_fallback is not a copy. Its seed-42 run recovers 12/12 equations for HypatiaX, against 10/12 in extrapolation.
    • Temperature runs: the fixn3 temp 0 and temp 0.25 runs are not identical, even though their sizes are almost equal. Every one of the ten runs recovers 12/12.
    • Partial files: none of the 25 _partial files is identical to its final file.
    • Duplicate case names: the DeFi cases include Collateral Ratio and Collateral ratio, and Reserve Ratio and Reserve ratio, as separate entries.
    • v3c2 header: hypatiax_defi_benchmark_v3c2_results.json says "Benchmark v4.0" inside, not v3c2.
    • v3c3 files: they hold 84 cases, not 74, under a single pure_llm key that also carries decision fields.
    • fixc3 baseline: its single parsed record says 74/90 passing (82%), next to a paper_claim of "9/30 = 0.300". I don't know whether those are meant to be the same quantity.
    • Seeds 77 and 777: both exist in the PCA DeFi runs and have different contents. So seed77 is a real separate run, not a renamed copy. Your run_all.sh has no 77, so I can't tell where it came from.
Limits
    • Six files in exp1_ablation are .json files that actually contain logs or CSV text. I flagged them but didn't parse them.
    • About 20 other JSONs (provenance maps, hybrid outputs, paper numbers and similar) have no typed table. Their values are in the raw-values table only.
    • CSV files are listed in the inventory but not loaded.
    • Where a file's experiment was a guess from its name (the two sparse-seed folders and benchmark_results.json), the mapping_basis column says so.
