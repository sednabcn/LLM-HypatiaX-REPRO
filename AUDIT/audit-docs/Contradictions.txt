Here's a list of inconsistencies and bugs I found on top of the many the paper already self-flags (the "flagged"/"corrected"/"withdrawn" apparatus is extensive, so I focused on things that survived it):
New numerical/data inconsistencies not caught by the paper's own corrections
    1. Nguyen-12 Table 13 doesn't support its own headline numbers. Counting the per-equation rows myself: PySR-only passes (extrap R² ≥ 0.9999) on N‑1,2,5,6,8,9,10,11 = 8/12, not the stated "10/12 (83.3%)". HypatiaX passes on N‑1,2,5,6,8,9,11 = 7/12, not the stated "11/12 (91.7%)." The table even contains a footnote claiming "strict recovery (≥0.9999) yields 4/12 (33.3%)" — a third number that also doesn't match the visible per-equation data. Three mutually inconsistent success counts (7, 4, and the reported 11) for the same table. 
    2. Supplementary Table 4 contradicts the Abstract/Table 9/Conclusion on M4's noiseless performance. Table 4 lists HLLMNN (M4) at "30/30 (v2)†" (100%), while the Abstract, Table 9, and Conclusion §14 all state M4 achieves 27/30 (90.0%) at the strict noiseless threshold, naming three specific failures (Lorentz force, Photon energy, Zeeman energy). Table 4 appears to be stale data never reconciled with the rest of the document. 
    3. EHSDeFi (M3) runtime is inconsistent by ~40×. Table 4 lists M3's average runtime as 20.2 s, but Table 10 (noise sweep) reports M3's average time at σ=0% as 841.4 s for what should be the same noiseless condition. The 841s figure is the one consistent with the claimed "1.5–76×" M3/M4 speedup range; the 20.2s figure in Table 4 is not. 
    4. Statistically incompatible CI. Main paper Table 1 reports the NN extrapolation-error 95% CI as [1087%, 1456%] (mean 1231%, n=13). Supplementary Appendix G reports SD=1842% for the same data. A CI that narrow is inconsistent with that SD/n (back-calculating gives a CI closer to roughly [120%, 2340%]). 
Undefined/contradictory benchmark variants
    5. "68 of 74" LLM-routed tasks is quoted repeatedly (Abstract, §8.3, §9.4) but doesn't match most of Table 5. The per-seed breakdown shows 69/74 for four of five v3c seeds (only v3c-seed123 and PCA-seed42 show 68/74). The paper never says which seed/split the oft-repeated "68 of 74" actually refers to. 
    6. "v3c" split is never defined. §6.4 describes exactly one splitting methodology (PCA-directed, aggressive 40/60), but Table 5/6 treat "v3c (aggressive 40/60)" and "PCA-directed (40/60)" as two distinct, equally-weighted benchmark variants. It's unclear which one (if either, or both) underlies the headline 90.5%/60.8% accuracy results in Tables 2–4. 
    7. Difficulty-tier arithmetic in Remark 9 doesn't add up. Going from v2.0 (24/27/20) to v3.0 (24/29/21) is +2 Medium, +1 Hard — but the text claims the three new v3.0 cases are all "additional hard cases in multi-asset risk," which would require +3 Hard, +0 Medium. 
    8. Figure 3's "DeFi AMM (Hard)" cell has no traceable source. The caption claims Table 8 gives "the per-equation values underlying this aggregate," but none of the Core-15 DeFi AMM equations (Constant Product, Impermanent Loss, Price Impact) have a Far-R² of −78.04 in Table 8. Also, §6.2 explicitly classifies Impermanent Loss as Medium, not Hard, creating a direct clash with Figure 3/4's implied classification. 
Unresolved gaps in the paper's own correction logic
    9. The corrected 60.8% success rate is computed, but a corrected mean R² never is. Table 2's headline mean R² (+0.8721) is still the uncorrected, masked-catastrophic-failure number. Given the paper's own disclosure that at least one masked sub-method failure has a true R² ≈ −141,000, a properly corrected mean could be enormously negative — this is never computed or addressed, even though "mean R²" is one of the paper's primary reported metrics. 
Smaller loose ends
    10. Reproducibility appendix self-contradicts on timeouts. §3.2 states PySR timeout = 1100 s; Appendix D's "exact reproduction command" uses --pysr-timeout 900. Separately, Appendix D says Arrhenius ran under a 300 s method-timeout limit yet "hung for 27574 s" before being killed — internally contradictory if the timeout was actually enforced. 
    11. Model/temperature mismatch across documents. Supplementary Appendix E specifies the Pure LLM model as claude-sonnet-4-20250514 at temperature 0.0, while the main paper (§10.9) says the actual Nguyen-12 code path calls claude-sonnet-5 at temperature 0.25 — one of at least five different hardcoded model strings found across the codebase for what's supposed to be a single pinned model (the paper flags this generally as FIX-N3 but never reconciles the Appendix E table specifically). 
    12. Minor rounding slippage in Tables 5/6. Several "speedup" values don't precisely reproduce from the rounded mean times shown in the same row (e.g., PCA pooled: 1.21/0.36 = 3.36×, printed as 3.40×) — small, but suggests the "mechanically regenerated, no manual editing" table wasn't cross-checked at full precision. 
    13. Near-duplicate task names across difficulty tiers in the §10.4 fabricated-success list: "Liquidation Price Long/Short" (Medium) vs. "Liquidation price for leveraged long/short" (Hard) — plausibly distinct tasks, but the naming overlap invites miscategorization/double-counting concerns that aren't addressed.
