Across all 74 tasks, HypatiaX achieves a median test $\Rsq$ of $1.00$ and a
near-perfect success rate ($\Rsq>0.99$) of \textbf{90.5\,\%}\footnote{This
figure is computed from the hybrid system's own reported \texttt{success}/$\Rsq$
fields without cross-checking the routing decision against the actual success
of the sub-method it names. A code-level attribution bug inflates it; the
corrected figure is 60.8\,\% (45/74), verified directly against
\texttt{hypatiax\_defi\_benchmark\_v3\_results\_seed42.json}. See
\S\ref{sec:hybrid-attribution-bug}.}---a $+28.3$
percentage point gain over the LLM baseline and $+85.1$\,pp over the neural
network---while \textbf{eliminating all catastrophic failures in HypatiaX's
own reported output}\footnote{The underlying computation is not free of
catastrophic failures: 22 of the 74 tasks route to a sub-method (typically
the LLM proposal) that itself failed catastrophically (e.g.\
\texttt{pure\_llm.test\_r2} $\approx -141{,}000$ on Loan-to-Value), but the
hybrid system's own accounting reports \texttt{success=True},
$\Rsq \approx 1.0$ regardless. The catastrophic failure is masked, not
eliminated. See \S\ref{sec:hybrid-attribution-bug}.}.
