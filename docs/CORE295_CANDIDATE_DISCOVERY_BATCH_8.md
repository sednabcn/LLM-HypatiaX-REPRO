## Cross-family overlap controls

- Maxwell’s four equations are a coupled field system. The source-free wave equation, wave speed and Poynting vector are derived consequences, not four extra independent equation families.
- Thin-lens and lens-maker equations share a derivation lineage through spherical refraction and the thin-lens approximation. Preserve sign conventions and do not count air-specific reductions separately.
- The double-slit fringe condition and diffraction-grating condition share the same path-difference equation. A finite slit envelope is an additional factor in the intensity pattern, not a separate copy of the fringe equation.
- Compton shift uses energy/momentum conservation and the photon relations already encountered in the quantum source universe. Treat the full collision model as one source family.
- The time-dependent Schrödinger PDE, time-independent eigenproblem, free-particle solution and quantum harmonic oscillator spectrum are related levels/solutions of the same operator framework. They may support distinct benchmark tasks only if the mathematical object and output mapping are explicitly distinguished; they are not automatically independent statistical units.
- Core-15 exclusions remain active. No kinetic-energy formula or gravitational-force formula is reintroduced by changing context or notation.

## Benchmarkability requirements

Vector fields, PDEs, eigenvalue problems and diffraction intensity profiles need explicit deterministic input/output adapters. Record domain geometry, boundary conditions, initial conditions, units, admissible parameter ranges, sign conventions, approximation regime and observable. A formula copied from a textbook is not automatically a scalar regression benchmark.
