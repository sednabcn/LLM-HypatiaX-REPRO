# Core-295 literature and source bibliography

**Purpose.** This is the consolidated bibliography for the Core-295 source-universe plan and discovery batches 1–3. It includes textbooks, primary scientific papers, protocol whitepapers/specifications, official documentation, standards and review literature. The references are discovery/provenance sources; listing a reference does **not** approve an equation, establish novelty, or certify statistical independence.

**Citation policy.** Every eventual catalogue entry must cite the most authoritative source for the exact equation (prefer the original paper, textbook equation, protocol whitepaper or versioned specification). Record page/chapter/section/equation number, edition/version, access date where relevant, assumptions, units and source-family ID in the entry itself. Review papers and surveys are discovery indexes: trace selected equations to their cited primary source. Official developer documentation can change; pin protocol version, contract/specification commit or archived version where possible.

## Bibliographic and provenance cautions

- Items 8–12 and 29, 36, 38–39 include reviews, teaching resources, empirical studies or risk frameworks. They can guide discovery but are not automatically authoritative formula sources.
- Items 18–28 and 30–34 are protocol/technical sources. Record the precise protocol version, contract release, whitepaper revision and fixed-point conventions.
- Core-15 exclusions apply even when the same equation appears in a new source. In particular: Arrhenius, Henderson–Hasselbalch, Core-15 rate law, allometric scaling, Michaelis–Menten, logistic growth, kinetic energy, gravitational force, ideal gas law, impermanent loss, price impact, constant product, VaR, liquidation price and portfolio standard deviation.
- The tumor-dynamics review's Eq. 5 logistic model is therefore excluded. Its other tumor model equations are only discovery leads until original references, assumptions and valid ranges are checked.
- Equations from one textbook, review, protocol invariant or derivation family must not be assumed to be independent statistical units. Maintain distinct `source_family_id` and `independence_unit_id`.
- Verify exact edition/revision and page-level metadata for book and protocol references before converting this discovery bibliography into a formal manuscript reference list.
