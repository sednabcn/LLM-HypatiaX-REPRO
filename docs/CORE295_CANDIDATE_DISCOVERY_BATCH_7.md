## Cross-protocol overlap controls

- Aave health factor, weighted collateralization ratio and borrowing-capacity calculations share a collateral/debt aggregation family. The Core-15 liquidation-price exclusion also prevents reintroducing that formula under a different name.
- Aave V3 and Compound III utilization-kink interest schedules are piecewise-affine rate functions with analogous low/high utilization slopes. Treat protocol implementations as related samples, not automatically independent mathematical families.
- Compound III's borrow and supply rates are related functions of the same utilization state. Annualized APR/APY conversions and fixed-point scaling are not additional equations.
- Compound III liquidation eligibility, liquidation factor, storefront discount and quote conversion are separate implementation steps, but only the documented nonlinear/threshold mapping may qualify as a distinct candidate after exact code-level transcription.
- Maker/Sky linear and exponential auction decay are alternative price-time schedules within the same Dutch-auction source family. The historical MIP45 is obsolete; current deployed calculator code and governance parameters must be pinned.
- Do not count liquidation price, collateralization ratio, health factor or VaR variants as novel merely because variable names or protocol interfaces differ. Core-15 exclusions and source-family clustering still apply.

## Acceptance requirements

For any selected candidate, capture the exact deployed contract or documentation revision, fixed-point units (WAD/RAY/RAD as applicable), oracle price units, utilization definition, governance parameters, rounding direction, boundary conditions, eligibility threshold and deterministic output. A protocol formula is not accepted merely because it appears in documentation. It must have a source-family ID, an independence-unit decision and an explicit benchmark input/output mapping.

All entries remain candidates. No experiments, live protocol calls or workflows have been run.
